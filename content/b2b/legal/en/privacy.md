This Privacy Policy explains how Tijwa ("we", "us") collects, uses and protects personal data when you use our platform as a business user. It applies under Kenya's Data Protection Act, 2019 (DPA) and, for users in the EU/EEA, the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

This policy covers data about **you and your business** as a Tijwa user. For how Tijwa handles **your customers' data** when you use the platform, see the data processing terms in our [Terms of Service](/legal/terms).

## 1. What we collect

- **Account information** — your name, email address, and password (stored as a hash)
- **Workspace configuration** — business name, logo, business type, operating hours, capabilities, and settings you configure
- **Conversation and contact data** — messages, contacts, and files you and your team store in the workspace (processed as your data processor)
- **Usage data** — pages visited, features used, actions taken, and timestamps, for security and product improvement
- **Technical data** — IP address, browser type, device information, and session identifiers
- **Billing information** — subscription plan, payment status, and transaction records (processed by our payment provider; we do not store card numbers)

## 2. Why we use it

- **To provide the Service** — account authentication, workspace management, and all CRM features (performance of a contract)
- **To process customer conversations on your behalf** — as your data processor, handling WhatsApp messages, contacts, and files you store (your instructions as controller)
- **To bill you and manage subscriptions** — payment processing, invoicing, and plan management (legal obligation / contract)
- **To secure the Service** — authentication, fraud prevention, access controls, and audit logging (legitimate interests)
- **To improve the Service** — usage analytics and feature development, aggregated and anonymised where possible (legitimate interests)
- **To communicate with you** — service notifications, security alerts, and (with your opt-in) product updates (legitimate interests / consent for marketing)

## 3. How long we keep it

- **Account data** — kept while your account is active, then deleted within 30 days of closure unless you request earlier deletion
- **Workspace data** (conversations, contacts, files) — kept while your workspace is active; deleted when you delete it or close your account
- **Billing records** — kept as required by Kenyan tax and accounting law (typically up to 5 years)
- **Security logs** — kept for up to 12 months for incident investigation
- **Marketing consent** — until you withdraw it

## 4. Who we share it with

- **Cloud infrastructure (Supabase)** — hosting and database services, under data processing terms
- **AI providers** — message content is sent to our AI model to generate replies, under processor terms; not used to train public models
- **WhatsApp / Meta** — to deliver messages you send through the Service (Meta is an independent controller for its own processing)
- **Payment processor** — for subscription billing
- **Authorities** — where required by law

We do not sell your personal data or your customers' personal data to anyone.

## 5. International transfers

Your data may be processed outside Kenya, including by our hosting and AI providers. Where data leaves Kenya, we rely on the safeguards required by the DPA 2019. Where data leaves the EU/EEA, we rely on adequacy decisions or standard contractual clauses.

## 6. Your rights

Under the DPA and, for EU/EEA users, the GDPR, you can:

- Request a **copy** of the personal data we hold about you (as a business user)
- Request **correction** of inaccurate data
- Request **deletion** of your account and personal data
- **Object to** or **restrict** certain processing
- Request data in a **portable** format
- **Withdraw consent** at any time (e.g. marketing emails)
- Complain to a supervisory authority — in Kenya, the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (ODPC)

To exercise these rights, use the settings in your workspace or contact us through the support system.

## 7. Security

We protect your data with access controls, encryption in transit (TLS) and at rest, multi-tenant isolation between workspaces, and audit logging of staff access. If a breach occurs that affects your rights we will notify you and the ODPC as required by the DPA 2019.

## 8. Children

The Service is not directed at children under 18, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

## 9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be notified by email or in-app at least 14 days before they take effect.

## 10. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy or your data? Contact us through the support ticket system in your workspace.
