Quick replies are reusable snippets — plain text or full interactive messages (buttons, lists) — that any team member can drop into a conversation in one action. They're shared across the whole workspace, so when one person writes a great reply, everyone benefits.

## The two ways to use a quick reply

### 1. Slash command (fastest)

In the message composer, type `/` on a line by itself. A menu appears showing every saved reply, filtered live as you keep typing:

- `/del` matches "Delivery information"
- `/price` matches "Pricing and quotes"

Navigate with **↑** and **↓**, then press **Enter** to insert:

- **Text replies** replace the `/query` line with the saved message, ready to edit before sending.
- **Interactive replies** open the interactive builder pre-filled with the saved buttons/list — tweak if needed, then send.

Press **Escape** to dismiss the menu without inserting anything.

### 2. The + menu

Tap the **+** button in the composer and choose **Quick reply**. A picker dialog lists all saved replies (with a preview of interactive ones). Click one and it's added to your draft — text appends to whatever you'd already typed; interactive replies open the builder instead. This path works offline too: replies are cached locally and sync in the background.

## Saving a reply while you compose

When you've built an interactive message you know you'll reuse (a "Track my order" button set, a common list menu):

1. Build it in the interactive builder as usual
2. Click **Save as quick reply** (next to the send controls)
3. Give it a memorable title when prompted

It's saved instantly for the whole team.

## Linking a reply to a Flow

A quick reply can kick off an automation: link it to a **Flow** and when the customer taps a button in that interactive message, the flow starts. Manage this under **Settings → Quick replies** — open a reply and pick a flow from the dropdown.

## Managing your replies

**Settings → Quick replies** is the full manager:

- Create new text or interactive replies
- Rename, edit content, or switch a reply's kind
- Link or unlink flows
- Delete replies you no longer need

Remember: quick replies are **workspace-wide**, not personal. Deleting one removes it for everyone, so check with your team first.
