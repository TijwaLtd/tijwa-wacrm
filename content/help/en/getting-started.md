Welcome! This guide walks you through the five things every new workspace does in the first hour. You can do them in any order, but the sequence below is the fastest path to a working WhatsApp CRM.

## 1. Connect your WhatsApp number

Go to **Settings → WhatsApp** and follow the connection flow. You'll need:

- A phone number that can receive a verification code
- The WhatsApp Business app (or WhatsApp) installed on that phone
- Admin access to a Meta Business account (or ability to create one)

Once connected, inbound messages from customers start landing in your **Inbox** automatically. Outbound messages you send from the CRM appear on the phone too — it's one continuous conversation.

## 2. Pick your business type

Open **Settings → Business** and choose the type that best describes your organization (Restaurant, Retailer, Hotel, NGO, Courier, and more). This is the single most impactful choice you'll make: it decides which **capabilities** get switched on for your workspace — catalog, orders, bookings, menu, delivery, applications, and so on.

You can see exactly what each type includes on the [Business types](/help/business-types) page. Changing your type later is safe — nothing is deleted, the feature set just adjusts.

## 3. Invite your team

Go to **Team** and send an invite. Roles work like this:

- **Owner** — full access, billing, cannot be removed by others
- **Admin** — everything except ownership transfers
- **Agent** — day-to-day work: inbox, contacts, catalogs, broadcasts
- **Viewer** — read-only; can still raise support tickets

Everyone shares the same inbox, contacts, and catalog — the CRM is a team workspace by design.

## 4. Add your catalog

Depending on your business type you'll see **Catalog** (products/services), **Rooms**, **Menu**, **Courses**, **Programs**, or **Property listings** — all backed by the same universal catalog engine. Add a few items with prices and photos. Once your catalog exists, the AI agent can answer "what do you sell?" and "how much is X?" in WhatsApp automatically, and customers can browse it from your profile page.

## 5. Say hello to the AI agent

The AI agent handles the repetitive conversations: answering questions from your knowledge base, showing the catalog, taking orders and bookings, and handing off to a human when it's unsure. Give it material to work with:

- Add FAQs and policies under **Knowledge**
- Toggle capabilities under **Settings → Business** so it knows what's live
- Review **Automations** for welcome messages and off-hours replies

That's it — you're live. When something confuses you (or the agent), hit **Help & support** in the sidebar and raise a ticket; every ticket gets a tracking ID like `TCK-7F3K2A`.
