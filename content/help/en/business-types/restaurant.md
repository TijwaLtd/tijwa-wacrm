---
title: Restaurant
description: Menu, food orders, and table reservations over WhatsApp
---

Restaurants live and die by fast, accurate order-taking — and by not missing a reservation call. This type sets you up for both.

Your customers can browse the full menu with photos and prices right inside WhatsApp, place orders that land in your **Food orders** queue with the kitchen-ready details, and book tables that appear on a shared **Reservations** board. The AI agent handles the "what's in the jollof rice?" and "can I get delivery to Westlands?" questions at any hour, and only pings your team when a human is genuinely needed — large catering requests, complaints, or anything the knowledge base can't answer.

Turn on **Events** if you run themed nights or private catering; the events capability adds announcements and registrations on top of the same catalog.
