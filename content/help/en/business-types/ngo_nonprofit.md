title: NGO / Nonprofit
description: Programs, applications, events, and donations — no products required
---

Running an NGO on WhatsApp usually means chaos: application forms in DMs, event RSVPs scattered across groups, donation confirmations in someone's personal inbox. This type replaces all of that with structured tools — and deliberately includes zero product or inventory features.

**Programs** and **NGO services** let people discover what you run and express interest in seconds. **Applications** turns those expressions into tracked intake — submitted, under review, accepted — instead of chat messages you can't query. **Events** handles mobilizations and RSVPs. **Resources** shares documents and information. **Donations** tracks pledges and confirmations.

The AI agent acts as your front desk: explaining programs, checking application status, and pointing people to the right form, 24/7. Add your FAQs and program descriptions under **Knowledge** and it gets smarter every week.
