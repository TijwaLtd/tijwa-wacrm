Your business type decides which features switch on out of the box. It's a starting point, not a cage — every capability can be toggled later under **Settings → Business**.

Pick your type below to see exactly which capabilities it enables and what each one does. Types with similar needs share the same foundations: retail and wholesale both get catalog and orders; restaurants, hotels, and event organizers all get bookings; service businesses of every kind get appointments.
