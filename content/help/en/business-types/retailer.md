---
title: Retailer
description: Product catalog, inventory, and orders over WhatsApp
---

Retail on WhatsApp means customers message "do you have X?" and "how much?" a hundred times a day. This type makes the AI agent answer all of that instantly, from a catalog you control.

You get a full **Catalog** with photos, prices, and stock levels, plus an **Inventory** view so you always know what's running low. Customer requests become structured **Orders** — pending, confirmed, shipped, delivered — instead of being buried in chat scrollback. **Inquiries** catch the questions that aren't orders (wholesale pricing requests, delivery areas, product comparisons) so nothing gets lost.

Upgrade to **Wholesale** as your business type if bulk pricing tiers become central — it adds price-list capabilities on top of everything here.
