## The plans

Every workspace starts on **Starter — free forever**, no credit card required. Paid plans unlock higher limits and priority support:

- **Starter (Free)** — enough for a small operation finding its feet: core inbox, AI agent, catalog, and a starter allowance of contacts, team members, and broadcasts per month.
- **Growth** — for teams past the trial stage: higher contact and broadcast limits, more team seats, priority queue on support tickets.
- **Business** — for established operations: the highest limits, additional automation capacity, and the fastest support response.

Exact numbers live on the [Plans page](/plans) — they're also shown in **Billing** inside the app, next to your current usage.

## Trials and the free plan

There's no time-limited trial — the free plan *is* the trial. Use it for as long as it fits. When you hit a limit (see below), upgrade; when you don't, stay free with a clear conscience.

## How limits work

Each plan caps a few resources: contacts synced, team members, broadcasts per month, and automation runs. The system tracks usage against these caps:

- You'll see usage meters in **Billing** so nothing is a surprise.
- Owners and admins get notified by email before a limit is reached.
- Hitting a limit **never deletes data or locks you out**. The workspace keeps working; only the specific capped action (syncing new contacts, sending the next broadcast) waits until you upgrade or free up space.

## Upgrading

1. Open **Billing** in the sidebar
2. Pick the plan you want
3. Pay by card — access is instant, limits recompute immediately

Downgrades work the same way and take effect at the end of the current billing period.

## Invoices and receipts

Payment confirmations are emailed to workspace owners. Every payment is also listed under **Billing → History** inside the app with a downloadable receipt.

## Cancelling

Cancel any time from **Billing**. Your workspace keeps working until the end of the paid period, then drops to Starter limits — nothing is deleted. If you're canceling because something didn't work, please raise a support ticket first; we'd rather fix it.

## Who can manage billing?

**Owners** see the full billing section. Admins can view plans and history but payment methods and plan changes are owner-only.

## Getting help with billing

Billing question, unexpected charge, invoice for your accountant — raise a ticket under **Help & support** with category **Billing** and your tracking ID will route it straight to the right person.
